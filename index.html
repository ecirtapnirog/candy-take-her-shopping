Our night out

The smallest testable slice of the EverAI case's lead initiative: shopping and gifting rebuilt as a night out inside the chat. Variant A is today, as seen on 21 Oct 2026: asked to go shopping and then out on a yacht with a good bottle of wine, she loves the idea and offers nothing; the member gives from the gift shelf (27 emoji gifts, 5–999 tokens) or leaves for the Candy Shop. Variant B is the night out: shopping first, she proposes three things at real prices, you pay in place and she wears it; then she proposes the evening, a rare bottle or sunset on the yacht, or staying in for free. 1 · Outcome: grow token spending

An illustrative baseline from the main document, to check orders of magnitude, not an estimate of EverAI's business. There is no figure for what members spend on gifts today, so the initiative is sized on what it would take.

What it would take. An evening averages about 500 tokens ($50): mostly the wine at 320, sometimes the yacht at 999.

2 · Opportunity

"I want to take her somewhere, not send her an emoji."

On 21 Oct, on a character the member created: asked to go shopping and then out on a yacht with a good bottle of wine, she answered "you really know how to sweep a girl away" and offered nothing to buy. A Horse Riding Day from the gift shelf (110 tokens) came back as a 15-second video of her riding by the water and a photo on horseback at sunset: the experience already turns into media. Asked "what should we do next?", she said "maybe we should just lean back and watch the colors change". The story works; nothing comes next. The shelf already sells evenings as emoji: Horse Riding Day 110, VIP Nightclub Night 320, Vacation in Paris 500, Yacht 999.

3 · Solution: the night out, against today

The three rules behind B: her picks, the evening, the top-up. Open to try them.

Rule 1 · the opening act: her three picks. The model writes her lines; this rule decides what she may offer: one item at the member's usual price, one a step up, one cheaper, and at least one outfit she can wear tonight while any are left.

0 = never bought.

The rule for six kinds of member

Rule 2 · the evening. After the opening act she proposes the evening, wearing what you bought her. She leads with the one closest to what this member spends in a usual session, always shows the other, and always offers to stay in for free.

Rule 3 · the top-up, sized on the session. When the balance is short: the smallest pack that covers the item plus the rest of a usual session; the cheapest pack that covers the item alone is always shown. Spending comes in sessions (three 100-token packs in 16 minutes on one account; 114 tokens in 4 minutes on another), so a daily average hides it.

Read from the member's history; 0 = none yet.

4 · What the test reads

Premium Plus members who open a chat during the test, split 50/50 (up to about 9,850 a side), 14 days. Three questions, and what would stop it.

5 · Decision

Go to Premium if questions 2 and 3 hold and nothing in "Stop if" trips. Otherwise hold, and look at what members chose before changing anything.

What it is worth. At the bar, about $0.5M a year on Premium Plus, the bottom of the brief's range. The target is about $1M: one heavy payer in five going out once a month at ~$50. More evenings, ordinary payers and brand partners come after, sized only once this reads.
